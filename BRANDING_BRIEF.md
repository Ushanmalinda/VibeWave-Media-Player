## Core Features (Key Selling Points)

### Media Playback
- ✅ Audio playback (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, OPUS)
- ✅ Video playback (MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WMV)
- ✅ High-quality audio rendering
- ✅ Video thumbnail generation

### Advanced Controls
- ✅ **Gesture Controls**: Swipe, double-tap, scroll gestures on album art
- ✅ **Hardware Controls**: Volume buttons, headset controls, shake detection
- ✅ **Shake to Skip**: Directional shake (left for previous, right for next)
- ✅ **Volume Swipe**: Vertical swipe for instant volume control
- ✅ **Smart Gestures**: Left/right double-tap for rewind/forward

### Audio Features
- ✅ Built-in equalizer with presets (Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, etc.)
- ✅ Bass boost and virtualization
- ✅ Audio session management
- ✅ Background playback support

### Organization
- ✅ Playlist management (create, edit, delete)
- ✅ Queue system with reordering
- ✅ Favorites/Liked songs
- ✅ Bookmarks for quick access
- ✅ Search functionality across all media

### User Experience
- ✅ Dark theme with orange accents
- ✅ Smooth animations and transitions
- ✅ Album art display with blur effects
- ✅ Mini player for quick control
- ✅ Customizable gesture actions
- ✅ Permission handling for storage access

### Technical Highlights
- ✅ Modern Flutter architecture
- ✅ State management with Provider
- ✅ Persistent settings with SharedPreferences
- ✅ File picker integration
- ✅ Sensor integration (accelerometer)
- ✅ Android API 33 support

## Design Direction for Logo

### Style Preferences
- **Modern & Minimal**: Clean lines, not overly complex
- **Memorable**: Recognizable at small sizes (app icon)
- **Professional**: Suitable for a serious media application
- **Energetic**: Suggests dynamic audio/video experience

### Visual Elements to Consider
- 🎵 Musical note or waveform
- ▶️ Play button symbolism
- 🎧 Headphones or audio elements
- 📊 Equalizer bars
- 🌊 Sound waves or frequency patterns
- 🎬 Video/media elements
- 🔊 Speaker or volume symbols
- ⚡ Energy/dynamic movement

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
- **Orange**: `#FF9800` (Main brand color - energetic, warm, creative)
- **Dark Gray/Black**: `#1a1a1a` to `#252525` (Background, sophistication)

#### Accent Colors
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` (Text, contrast)
- **Medium Gray**: `#333333` to `#555555` (Secondary elements)
- **Light Orange**: `#FFB74D` (Highlights, hover states)

#### Alternative Palette Suggestions
- Orange + Deep Purple (creative, artistic)
- Orange + Teal (modern, balanced)
- Orange + Navy Blue (professional, trustworthy)

## Logo Specifications

### Technical Requirements
- **App Icon Size**: 1024x1024px (high resolution)
- **Formats Needed**:
  - PNG with transparency
  - SVG (vector, scalable)
  - Adaptive icon versions (Android)
- **Adaptive Icon**: Foreground + Background layers for Android
- **Safe Zone**: Keep important elements within 80% of canvas
- **Platform Compatibility**: Android (primary), iOS, Web

### Icon Variations Needed
1. **Full Color** (main app icon)
2. **Monochrome** (notification bar, system UI)
3. **Light Theme** variant (if needed)
4. **Dark Theme** variant (if needed)

### Design Constraints
- ❌ Avoid: Overly detailed designs (poor readability at small sizes)
- ❌ Avoid: Generic stock icons
- ❌ Avoid: Text-heavy logos (hard to read on mobile)
- ✅ Prefer: Simple, bold shapes
- ✅ Prefer: High contrast elements
- ✅ Prefer: Unique, distinctive design

## Testing Checklist

Once you receive logo designs:

### Visual Tests
- [ ] Looks good at 48x48px (smallest size)
- [ ] Looks good at 512x512px (Play Store)
- [ ] Recognizable in monochrome
- [ ] Distinctive from competitors
- [ ] Works on light and dark backgrounds
- [ ] No pixelation or artifacts

### Technical Tests
- [ ] PNG has transparent background
- [ ] SVG scales without quality loss
- [ ] Adaptive icon layers separated correctly
- [ ] Colors match brand palette
- [ ] Files under 1MB for app bundle

### Brand Tests
- [ ] Reflects app's modern, powerful personality
- [ ] Appeals to target audience
- [ ] Memorable and unique
- [ ] Communicates "media player" purpose
- [ ] Professional quality
